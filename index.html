The problem

Every grant begins with a question.

Then the paperwork starts: a notice of intent, then a proposal with budgets, rates, federal forms and portal submissions, then an award to administer. Proposa exists to unshackle the PI from all of it.

ARCH-IE is the AI operating system behind everything Bryant Systems builds. Inside Proposa it becomes your organization’s own AI: it learns your rates, templates and past proposals, and it runs on hardware you control.
