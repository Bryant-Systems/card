The problem02 / 08

Every federal proposal starts with a budget.

Before a researcher can submit, someone in finance prices the work, applies the institution’s rates, fills out the federal budget forms, and enters it all again in the agency’s portal.

That is at least two hours of a financial manager’s time for every proposal built from scratch.

Why I’m building this03 / 08

I’ve built these budgets by hand, hundreds of times.

From 2022 to 2026 I was a Space Sector Financial Manager at the Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory, behind hundreds of NASA and NSF proposal submissions.

Proposa is the tool I wanted on my desk: a seasoned financial manager’s judgement, available to every researcher on every proposal.
